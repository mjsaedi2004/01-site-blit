**Sample posters** (`posters/`). These stand in for what an organiser sends. They were designed for this prototype; the people, names and logos on them are fictional.

**Ready-made objects** (`emblems/`). These were rendered for this prototype with three.js (`../tools/emblems.html`).

**Files.** `people/` and `emblems/` are neutral transparent WebP; the page tints them in the show's colour. `posters/` are the files as an organiser would send them, plus 520px thumbnails for the featured cards.

**Fonts.** Noto Sans Arabic, Noto Nastaliq Urdu, Vazirmatn and Lalezar, all from Google Fonts under the SIL Open Font License.
