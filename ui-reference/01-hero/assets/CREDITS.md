# Credits

## Posters

`posters/*.webp` are original sample artwork made for this prototype. The source is `../posters/source.html` and `../posters/render.js` renders it. They stand in for the posters organisers will upload, and they use these fonts from Google Fonts, all under the SIL Open Font License:
- Lalezar
- Vazirmatn
- Noto Kufi Arabic
- Noto Nastaliq Urdu

Sizes:
- `name.webp`, 1200×1800: the stage and the poster viewer.
- `name-thumb.webp`, 400×600: the featured rail and the notes.
